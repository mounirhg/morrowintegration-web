Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data is collected through this website, why, and what you can do about it.

What this website collects on its own

Nothing. This site is a set of static files. It has no contact form, no login, no analytics script, no advertising pixel and no cookies of its own. It loads no fonts, scripts or resources from any third-party server: every file it uses is served from the site itself.

Your hosting provider may keep standard server access logs (IP address, date, requested page, browser). Those logs are kept by the provider, not by this site.

What happens when you contact us

When you call, text or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the phone number you contact us from and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it for one purpose: answering you and, if you engage us, delivering the work.

  • We do not sell, rent or trade contact details.
  • We do not add you to a marketing list because you contacted us once.
  • Messages sent over WhatsApp also pass through WhatsApp's own systems and are subject to its privacy policy.

Client information during an engagement

An audit necessarily involves looking at how a company works, which can include internal documents, systems and data. That information is used solely to produce the agreed deliverables, is not disclosed to third parties, and is not reused for any other client. A non-disclosure agreement is signed on every engagement by default, before any access to that information, and the specific terms are set out in the written agreement for each engagement. See the terms of service.

How long information is kept

Contact details and engagement records are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and accounting obligations, and are deleted after that.

Your rights

You can ask what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to mounir@morrowintegration.com — that is the channel that leaves a written record for both sides — or call or text +1 786 742 0580. We answer requests within 30 days of receiving them.

Children

These services are aimed at businesses. This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If the site or the way we handle information changes, this page is updated. The version published here is the one in force.
