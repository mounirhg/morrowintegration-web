3. What is not included

  • The audit does not implement anything. That is deliberate: the roadmap belongs to the client and any team can execute it, including one that is not Morrow Integration.
  • The cost of licences, subscriptions, infrastructure or third-party tools recommended by the roadmap is not included. The client contracts those in its own name.
  • Support and maintenance after delivery are not included unless engaged separately in writing.
  • The vulnerability study covers each implementation proposed in the roadmap and its scope: what access it needs, what data it touches, what providers and people it depends on, whether it can be audited and whether it can be switched off quickly. It is not a security audit of the client's company, not a review of its infrastructure, passwords or network, and not a penetration test.
